Part 3 Alignment and strand-specificity
---------------------------------------
To see if the library was stranded or not I looked at the percentage of
of reads mapped for forward and reverse. If the percentages are similar 
they are likely not stranded.

mapped for 385 fw:

$ grep -v '^__' Project2_Part3/htseq_385.txt | awk '{sum+=$2} END {print sum}'

mapped = 781064

total for 385 fw:

$ awk '{sum+=$2} END {print sum}' Project2_Part3/htseq_385.txt
 
total = 32872051

percentage mapped for 385 fw:** (781064/32872051) * 100 = 2.376%

mapped for 385 rv:
  
$ grep -v '^__' Project2_Part3/htseq_385_rv.txt | awk '{sum+=$2} END {print sum}'
 
mapped = 16425434

total for 385 rv:
  
$ awk '{sum+=$2} END {print sum}' Project2_Part3/htseq_385_rv.txt
 
total = 32872051

percentage mapped for 385 rv: (16425434/32872051) * 100 = 49.968%

mapped for 409 fw:
  
$ grep -v '^__' Project2_Part3/htseq_409.txt | awk '{sum+=$2} END {print sum}'
 
mapped = 1183962

total for 409 fw:
  
$ awk '{sum+=$2} END {print sum}' Project2_Part3/htseq_409.txt
 
total = 43054217

percentage mapped for 409 fw: (1183962/43054217) * 100 = 2.75%

mapped for 409 rv:
  
$ grep -v '^__' Project2_Part3/htseq_409_rv.txt | awk '{sum+=$2} END {print sum}'
 
mapped = 21609071

total for 409 rv:
  
$ awk '{sum+=$2} END {print sum}' Project2_Part3/htseq_409_rv.txt
 
total = 43054217

percentage mapped for 409 rv: (21609071/43054217) * 100 = 50.19%

Based on these results there is very disproportionate mapping between the forward 
and reverse strands for both files, indicating that there was strandedness during 
the RNAseq library prep for this data. Since the reverse files had the significantly 
higher amount of reads mapped, 49.968% for SRR25630385 and 50.19% for SRR25630409, 
these data are reverse-strand specific, and the stranded=reverse parameter is 
appropriate.
