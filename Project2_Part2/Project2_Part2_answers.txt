I looked in the slurm outs after running cutadapt and found info on percentage of reads that had adapters 
and were therefore trimmed:
Read 1 with adapter:               7,592,116 (22.9%)

Report the commands you used, the reasoning behind them, and how you confirmed the adapter sequences.

Based on the FASTQC outputs, there was a high likelihood that the adapters are the Illumina Universial adapters. Additionally
the methods from the dataset indicated that they used the Illumina HiSeq 4000 sequencing system, which confirmed this. 

Comment on whether you expect R1s and R2s to be adapter-trimmed at different rates and why.

I would expect R1 and R2 to be adapter trimmed at similar rates since they are still looking at the same read
just from opposite ends. I might expect R2 to be a little different due to R2 typically having a lower 
sequencing quality than R1 which could impact adapter detection.
